Sizing & delivery

What sizes do you stock?

Our range comes in EU sizes 39 to 43. Each product page shows live stock for every colour and size. If you're between sizes, message us and we'll help.

Do you ship across Australia?

Yes. Shipping is A$12 flat across Australia, and free on orders over A$150.

How do I pay?

Add your shoes to the cart and check out with card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Payments are handled securely by Stripe, and we never see your card details.

Can I exchange or return my shoes?

Get in touch with your order details and we'll explain the options for your pair.

Are your shoes vegan?

Styles with "Vegan" in the name are made without animal leather. Ask us about any other style and we'll tell you what it's made from.
